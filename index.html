Algorithmic Periodization

The L3X Engine autonomously manages your fatigue and progression curves just like an elite sports scientist. Here is how your training is structured:

The Macrocycle

The Master Strategy

The Macrocycle is your long-term roadmap. The engine automatically sequences your year into distinct physiological phases—Hypertrophy, Strength, Power, and Deload—ensuring you peak at the right time while continuously driving maximal adaptation.

Note: The automated macrocycle phase-shifting engine is unlocked for Advanced users only, as beginners and intermediates benefit most from continuous linear progression before requiring complex systemic shocks.

The Mesocycle

The Training Block

A Mesocycle is your current 4-to-6 week training block. During this phase, the L3X engine locks in your exercises week-over-week. This allows our algorithms to accurately track your neural pathways and autonomously micro-adjust your weights and reps to force progressive overload. When the engine detects a plateau, it automatically triggers a phase shift.

The RPE Feedback Loop: The Engine relies entirely on your Rate of Perceived Exertion (RPE). By accurately rating the subjective difficulty of every set, you provide the algorithm with the exact physiological feedback required to calculate your true fatigue limits and autonomously scale your weights for the next session.

The Microcycle

The Tactical Execution

The Microcycle is your week-to-week tactical execution. This is where your custom "X" vectors shine. The engine dynamically weaves your specialized sport protocols, active recovery days, and VO2 Max conditioning directly into your foundational week, ensuring you never overtrain your central nervous system.
